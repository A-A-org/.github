# School ERP SaaS

## Introduction

School ERP SaaS is a web-based school management system designed to help educational institutions manage their daily operations through one centralized platform.

The system allows schools to manage students, employees, attendance, fees, exams, and reports digitally.
## Problems
Why are we bulding these ?

Many schools still use manual paperwork, Excel sheets, and separate systems to manage their operations.

This creates problems such as:
- Data duplication
- Difficulty tracking student information
- Time-consuming reports
- Poor communication
- Lack of centralized management
